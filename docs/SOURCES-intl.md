## Scam intelligence (Netcraft, ESET, Help Net Security reports, Nov 2026)
Blocked: the `.icu` domain ending, mygta6.icu, gta6keycodes.com, gta6cyberleek.com. Rejected phrases: "complete offers", "human verification", "codes left today", "cyberleek", fake `.exe` and `.apk` names. Penalised phrases: "early access", "launcher", "sideload", "leaked version", "content locker". GTA 6 is only out on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S, so any PC, Android or "early access" giveaway is treated as a red flag.
